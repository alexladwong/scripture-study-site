Respectful by design

Christian in identity.
Open in invitation.

Scripture Atlas presents the Bible as Christian Scripture while distinguishing biblical text, historical context, tradition and interpretation. Visitors do not need to share the site's faith commitments to learn from it.

Biblical textReferences are shown directly and never disguised as commentary.
Historical contextEmpires, lands and routes are explained without pretending every detail is certain.
Christian interpretationFaith reflections are clearly presented as Christian theological reading.
Interfaith respectNo mocking, caricaturing or attacking other faiths.
