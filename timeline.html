Biblical History Timeline

A high-level teaching sequence that connects the atlas' places and empires across major biblical eras.

01 · Eden, Noah, Babel

Origins

The source begins with primeval narratives and ancient-world orientation.

02 · Abraham, Jacob, Joseph

Patriarchs

Journeys connect Mesopotamia, Canaan and Egypt.

03 · Egypt, Sinai, Wilderness

Exodus

The route is presented traditionally and geographically.

04 · Canaan, Tribes, Jericho

Settlement

The twelve tribes and surrounding nations become central.

05 · David, Solomon, Jerusalem

United Kingdom

Jerusalem and the Temple rise in prominence.

06 · Israel and Judah

Divided Kingdom

The source marks the political split after Solomon.

07 · Israel, Judah, Exile

Assyrian / Babylonian pressure

Imperial power reshapes the biblical landscape.

08 · Return and rebuilding

Persian period

Persia becomes the setting for return from exile.

09 · Alexander and Hellenistic influence

Greek world

Greek culture forms background to the New Testament.

10 · Jesus and early Church

Roman world

Rome frames the political world of the Gospels and Acts.
